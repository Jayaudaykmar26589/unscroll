# Predict corpus: source and licence

`corpus.json` is derived by `scripts/build_corpus.py` from Diogenes Laertius,
*Lives of Eminent Philosophers* (Greek), Perseus Digital Library,
[canonical-greekLit](https://github.com/PerseusDL/canonical-greekLit),
file `tlg0004.tlg001.perseus-grc2.xml` (copied unchanged in `raw/`).
Perseus releases this text under CC BY-SA 4.0. Because `corpus.json` adapts it
(split into sentences, normalised), `corpus.json` and `raw/` are shared under
the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution to the Perseus Digital Library.

The corpus also includes the published preliminary reading of PHerc. Paris. 4
column −8 (Vesuvius Challenge Grand Prize page, CC BY-NC 4.0).
