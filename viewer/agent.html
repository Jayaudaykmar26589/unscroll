THE AGENT, STEP BY STEP

Watch Muse Wonder extend a trace, then catch its own mistake.

SAM 3.1 stops when it loses the sheet, so long traces need re-seeding. A Muse Wonder agent does that by tool calling: it decides where to re-seed, spends a fixed budget of GPU runs on Kaggle, scores the result, and checks every seam with numbers plus Muse Spark looking at the two slices. This page replays its three real sessions on one 100-slice patch, call by call, exactly as logged. Nothing was edited: every message, argument and result below is from reports/agent/.

WHAT TO TAKE FROM IT

Autonomy: the agent chose every re-seed point, decided when to spend its limited GPU runs, and decided to exclude runs; no person intervened in these sessions. Effectiveness: with only extension tools (sessions 1 and 2) it grew the patch from 58 to 97 slices, but it could not tell that the growth was on the wrong sheet. Given a seam check (session 3), it found the first re-seed had jumped sheets, traced the two later runs back to it, excluded all three with written reasons, and kept the 58 slices that score 87.3% within 40 µm of the hand-traced layer. The lesson the project draws is that an agent needs a check it can run on its own output before its extra work can be trusted.
