EVALUATION ON REAL SCROLL 1 CT

Does the trace stay on the clicked sheet?

Each click sits exactly on a papyrus sheet that people traced by hand and released for Scroll 1 (PHerc. Paris. 4). SAM 3.1 follows it through 20 CT slices, and every trace is then measured against that hand-made trace, which the tracer never sees. 89 clicks: 15 used while building the fix, 74 never used.

What we found

Green: the sheet people traced. Red: Unscroll's trace. Yellow: the click. Before the fix the red line jumps to a brighter neighbouring sheet; after it, it stays. The last example is the one development click that got worse, shown deliberately.

Development clicks: before and after the fix

The first slice used to pick the brightest path through SAM's broad mask, which could cross a dark gap to a neighbouring sheet. The fix walks outward from the click, one voxel sideways at most per step. Same 15 clicks, same slices, same GPU setup. These clicks were used to build the fix; the sections below test it on new ones.

Held-out check

Fifteen new clicks on the same human-traced layers, about 700 voxels from the originals, never used while developing the fix. The fix was not changed after the development run.
