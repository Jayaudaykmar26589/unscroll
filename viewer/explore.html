THE RESULT, CLICK BY CLICK

Pick any of the 89 measured clicks and see what each tracer did.

Every click was placed on a sheet people traced by hand. Each was traced through 20 CT slices three ways: SAM 3.1 before the first-slice fix, after it, and a classic optical-flow tracer from the same start. Scrub the slices to watch them, read the exact distance to the hand-traced sheet on every slice, and download Unscroll's trace in the format the Vesuvius Challenge tools open. Nothing here is a demo image: the crops are the real CT and the numbers are the ones in the reports.
