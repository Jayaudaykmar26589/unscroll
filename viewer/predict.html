FRAGMENT → WHOLE SENTENCE

Give part of a sentence. Get the whole one, with its source.

Type any part of a Greek sentence: the beginning, the middle or the end, even with a word cut off as on a papyrus edge. Unscroll first looks for it in attested Greek text and returns the full sentence with its reference. Only when nothing is attested does Muse Spark propose completions, clearly marked as conjecture.

How accurate is it?

Corpus: Diogenes Laertius, Lives of Eminent Philosophers (Greek, Perseus Digital Library, CC BY-SA 4.0), including Epicurus' letters and Principal Doctrines, the school Philodemus wrote for, plus the published preliminary reading of PHerc. Paris. 4 col. −8. Attested results are only as good as that edition. Model conjectures are unreviewed suggestions, never readings of the scroll.
